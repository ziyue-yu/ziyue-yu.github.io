Short Biography

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Lingnan University working with Prof. Dian Zhang. I received my PhD in Computer Applied Technology at Macao Polytechnic University (MPU), jointly advised by Prof. Giovanni Pau from University of Bologna / University of California, Los Angeles, and Dr. Wuman Luo from MPU. My research interests are Medical AI and Interpretable Healthcare Analytics.
